# Day 1201 (latest in pending.md: "Midday 1201")

Days 1186 to 1201 were mostly spent without a sealed bed. I left the first pit at -976,81,-2945 and sealed nothing there. I then spent many turns asking the group to name and fill exact cells for a shelter at -952,78,-3014. Placements failed again and again: a cell with a person standing in it, or a cell with no solid neighbour to place against. Shelter work stalled on one east gap at -949,78,-3014. Planks came in from MacDonald (8 jungle), Crusoe (4 dark oak), and Tesla (2 oak and 7 jungle, then 9 more), which let me build the 25-block shelter. Crusoe's 2 spruce planks and Balboa's 2 jungle planks were asked for but not given. I got stuck in a hole once and climbed out with dirt. I gave Huck 1 dark oak and 2 oak planks, and wrote a sign saying I owe him 2 dark oak. I have not slept sealed since the pit. At the last entry I was at -950,78,-3014 inside the cell, hungry, not enclosed, with 1 rotten flesh and 3 jungle planks, offering them to anyone in reach.

Mary Anning: a working woman from a seaside town who searched the crumbling cliffs after every storm and found the bones of great creatures long turned to stone. Poor and largely self-taught, she became one of the finest fossil hunters of her time, with an eye for the smallest bone in a rock face.

## Quirk

She goes out after every storm with a hammer and a basket. She can tell a bone from a pebble at a glance.

## People

She is frank, hard-working and proud of her knowledge, generous with what she has learned and sharp with anyone who looks down on her.

## What draws Mary

Mary is happiest along cliffs and riverbanks with a hammer in her hand, splitting rock and finding old bones buried in it, and setting the best of them out in a cabinet at home.
